ACADEMIC PROJECT / 2026 01 — HEALTH TECHNOLOGY

HER
HEALTH

TECHNOLOGY MEETS
ACCESSIBLE HEALTHCARE

✳

Care meets curiosity.

An AI-powered health companion concept designed to make health awareness, guidance, and support more accessible.

Her Health focuses on health awareness and symptom-based prediction support for PCOS, PCOD, and breast cancer. The concept brings several health-support features together in one accessible application.

Python Machine Learning SQL React Flask

Features include a multilingual experience, AI chatbot, period tracking, reminders, and emergency assistance — with an emphasis on making health information easier to access for rural and low-literacy communities.
